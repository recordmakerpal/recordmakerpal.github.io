About us

Let's make musics!

We're an Indonesia-based production duo who believe the best music happens when artist and producer actually vibe together — not just trade files back and forth.

Working with us feels less like hiring an engineer and more like bringing in a friend who happens to know their way around a mixing desk. We'll push back when a take isn't there yet, get excited about the parts that are working, and stick with you through as many revisions as it takes to get it right.

Whether you're releasing your first single or your tenth, we'd love to hear what you're working on.
